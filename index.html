About

Systems first. Models second.

I’m a Staff Engineer at DISH Network Technologies in Bengaluru, with prior time at Morgan Stanley. Outside product work I dig into local inference, Metal kernels, and learning systems — the kind of code that runs close to the silicon.

These repos were built with strong assistance from coding agents, the same way serious systems work gets shipped now. I lead the ideas, the tests, and the debugging. I say that openly because it shaped how the work was made — you still have to know what to ask for, and what to throw away.
