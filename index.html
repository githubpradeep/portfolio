About

Systems first. Models second.

I’m a Staff Engineer at DISH Network Technologies in Bengaluru, with prior time at Morgan Stanley. Outside product work I dig into the metal — writing inference servers, GPU operators, and learning loops by hand so the abstractions stay honest.

The through-line across my repos is the same: understand the algorithm deep enough to implement it without the framework, then make it fast where the hardware actually lives.

Focus

  • Apple Metal / CUDA kernels
  • Reinforcement learning from scratch
